# Goals

Each goal is a check with a number, tied to a want or a risk in [PROPOSAL.md](PROPOSAL.md). A goal met on one project stays open for another until it is measured there. A status changes only with a measurement and its date. For goals measured on this repo, the reader who measures did not write the change.
