# 038. Two guidelines leave DESIGNS

Decided: 2026-11-04

## The problem

Two guidelines in DESIGNS derived from no PHILOSOPHY stance.

- **DESIGNS 3: No dependencies:** it followed from the choice that the skill is plain Markdown for any agent, which decisions 016 and 037 record.
- **DESIGNS 4: Our own words over borrowed text:** it steered how this repo writes the skill and not what the skill must be, and AGENTS.md already held it in *Before you finish*.

## The choice

Both leave DESIGNS, which keeps 4 guidelines. Examples the reader knows becomes DESIGNS 3 and Hard rules for the doc becomes DESIGNS 4, with every link changed in the same change. The *Not planned* item on check scripts in FEATURES now links to decision 037.

## Not chosen

- Keeping both with stronger reasons: neither reason came from a stance, and one was about how the work is done, which decision 012 moved to AGENTS.md.
- Linking No dependencies to the stance on the chain from one window to the next: the fit was forced.
- Keeping the old numbers with gaps: a numbered list renders its items in order, so the list would show the wrong numbers.

## Consequences

DESIGNS no longer links to decisions 004, 006 and 016. They stay as the record of where the rules came from.
