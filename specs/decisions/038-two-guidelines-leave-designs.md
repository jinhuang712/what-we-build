# 038. Two guidelines leave DESIGNS

Decided: 2026-11-04

## The problem

Two guidelines in DESIGNS derived from no PHILOSOPHY stance.

- **DESIGNS 3: No dependencies:** it followed from the choice that the skill is plain Markdown for any agent, which 016: A skill and no plugin and 037: The skill is written for any agent record.
- **DESIGNS 4: Our own words over borrowed text:** it steered how this repo writes the skill and not what the skill must be, and AGENTS.md already held it in *Before you finish*.

## The choice

Both leave DESIGNS, which keeps 4 guidelines. Examples the reader knows becomes DESIGNS 3 and Hard rules for the doc becomes DESIGNS 4, with every link changed in the same change. The *Not planned* item on check scripts in FEATURES now links to 037: The skill is written for any agent.

## Not chosen

- Keeping both with stronger reasons: neither reason came from a stance.
- Keeping Our own words with a stronger reason: it is about how the work is done, which 012: This repo's work rules leave DESIGNS moved to AGENTS.md.
- Linking No dependencies to the stance on the chain from one window to the next: the fit was forced.
- Keeping the old numbers with gaps: a numbered list renders its items in order, so the list would show the wrong numbers.

## Consequences

DESIGNS no longer links to 004: Our own skill over jig's `vision-elicitation`, 006: Three ideas from mattpocock/skills over the whole guide or 016: A skill and no plugin. They stay as the record of where the rules came from.
