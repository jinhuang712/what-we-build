# 006. Three ideas from mattpocock/skills over the whole guide

Decided: 2026-10-13

## The problem

The writing guidance in mattpocock/skills (MIT) is long. Only part of it matched a failure a session had shown.

## The choice

We take three ideas and rewrite them in our own words:

- State the target instead of the ban.
- Treat a doc that restates the code as a cache.
- Record only decisions that are hard to reverse, surprising and a real trade-off.

## Not chosen

- The whole guide: most of it fixes failures no session had shown.

## Consequences

Each idea taken traces to a failure a session showed.
