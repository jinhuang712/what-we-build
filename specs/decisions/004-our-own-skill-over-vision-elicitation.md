# 004. Our own skill over jig's `vision-elicitation`

Decided: 2026-10-12

## The problem

An existing skill already drafts a project's vision.

## The choice

We write our own skill and our own format.

## Not chosen

- jig's `vision-elicitation` (ramboz/jig, MIT): it runs a fixed 13-section questionnaire after jig's own scaffold.
  It fills two templates.
  It has no doc set and no push-back.
  It has no goals with numbers and no manual start.

## Consequences

Its per-section fill marks gave the idea for the doc marks.
