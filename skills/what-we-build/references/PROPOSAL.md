# PROPOSAL.md

The proposal is where the project begins: the case for why it should exist. It names the pain, the change that lets the pain be eased now, and the idea that answers it. A reader finishes it knowing whether the project is worth building.

It comes first, because every other doc builds on the pain it names.

**Owns:** the pain points, the shift, the idea, what we want, what we don't want, who it is for, the approach, the risks, and how we will know it works.

**Leaves out:** exact behavior (FEATURES), measured results (GOALS), progress (ROADMAP), and the index of docs (README).

**Push back on:** a case that opens with the solution, a pain with no example, a want that serves no pain, and a "don't want" with no reason.

## Guidelines

- **Start from the pain,** with a moment or a number that proves it.
- **Argue the case.** Every section should make it stronger.
- **Map every want to a pain,** and every pain to a want.

## Skeleton

```markdown

> <tagline>

<a scene the reader knows, then the one-line answer>

## Pain points

|---|---|
| <how it was> | <what changed> |

**<one sentence>.** <what it means>

## What we want
- **<what the reader needs>.** <how>

## How it works
<one concrete sample, a few lines>

## What we don't want
- **<what the project refuses to become>.** <the reason>

## Who it is for
<the people, and what each gets>

## Approach
<how we will build it, in a few lines>

## Risks

<the headline checks, linked to GOALS.md>
```
